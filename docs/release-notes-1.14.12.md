# Flashborn 1.14.12

Search fields no longer gain rectangular focus outlines. Regular and Flashcognito new-tab search pills and bolt tiles have no borders or shadows, and the top URL pill has no border, outline or shadow in either theme, including its focused state. The pill shapes, round Go buttons and arrow animations are retained.

The title, navigation and bottom status bars share the sidebar colour in both themes, with separator borders and shadows removed. This creates a continuous top, left and bottom frame with an open right edge. Native Flash placement and resizing are unchanged.

Verified regular and Flashcognito new tabs in light and dark themes, including clicking and typing in search fields, keyboard focus on the Go and library controls, and focus in the address and bookmark-filter fields. Screenshots and computed styles confirm borderless search pills and bolt tiles, no focus rectangles, and matching frame colours without separators.

The packaged build passed the same checks. Installed and launched for Vas, with all 69 packaged source files verified and the browser, collection and thumbnail files preserved byte-for-byte during installation.
