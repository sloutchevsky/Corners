# Corners 1.14.27

Corners now has a 160 px collapsed drawer, readable labels with ellipses, and unchanged tab thumbnail sizes. Browser navigation sits beside the URL field and slides horizontally with the drawer. Opening a new tab keeps navigation at the same vertical position. Special pages retain their compact controls.

Both resize corners slide diagonally out from underneath the browser, from a 5 px resting stroke to a full 15 px stroke on hover. Page scrollbars use the same 5-to-15 px motion outward; the tab scrollbar extends from the left edge. Controls stay extended during dragging, remain joined to the browser edge, and retain clear clickable tracks.

The transparent 15 px window perimeter, scrolling masks, menu layering and four themes are retained. App icons are unchanged.

Validation: automated navigation transitions, four-theme drawer/thumbnail checks, scrollbar motion and input checks, modern/Flash frame integration, full unit suite, package source verification, and packaged-app smoke checks.
